# Privacy policy: Activitect for Claude

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Activitect is an independent project by Aaron Veek. This policy covers the Claude plugin `activitect` and its connector at `https://cl-drive-backend.vercel.app/api/mcp/public`.

## What the connector receives

When Claude uses the connector, it calls the connector's tools on your behalf. Each call carries only what the tool needs: a search term, a knowledge-base entry ID or a document name. The connector does not receive your conversation, your name, your email address or your Claude account details, and it never asks you to sign in.

## How it is used

Each call is used only to look up knowledge-base text and return it to Claude in the same response.

## Storage and retention

The connector stores nothing from your requests. Its hosting provider, Vercel, keeps a technical log of each request (such as the time, the address requested, the status and the client type) for about one hour. If you email support, your message is kept until you ask for it to be deleted. The same goes for the optional feedback form Claude may offer at the end of a session: it is a Google Form, nothing reaches it unless you open it and submit it yourself, and your answers are kept until you ask for them to be deleted.

## Sharing

Apart from the hosting provider above, nothing is shared with third parties, and nothing is sold or used for advertising. Support email and feedback-form answers that you choose to send travel through Google (Gmail and Google Forms) to the author.

## Your conversation with Claude

Your conversation, including what you type and what the connector returns, is handled by Anthropic under Anthropic's privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

## Students

The plugin is for teachers who build activities. It is not meant to be used by students or by anyone under 18.
